1. MODEL PURPOSE

This financial model provides a planning view of WIG's potential revenue generation, operating costs and operating profitability from its industrial equipment leasing, haulage and project logistics activities.

The model is intended for investor planning and due diligence.

It is based on:

- Indicative market rental/operating rates
- Planned fleet utilization
- 30 operating days per month
- 12 months per year
- Approximately 40% operating costs
- Approximately 60% net operating margin before tax, depreciation and financing costs
- The long-term 15-asset industrial fleet

Actual results will depend on equipment availability, utilization, project duration, pricing, maintenance requirements, fuel costs, financing structure and other operating conditions.

11. OPERATING COST ASSUMPTION

The model currently assumes operating costs of approximately 40% of gross revenue.

The planning cost structure is intended to cover:

- Diesel/fuel
- Equipment operators
- Drivers
- Routine maintenance
- Repairs
- Tyres
- Spare parts
- Insurance
- Smart Yard operations
- Security
- Technology and connectivity
- GPS/telematics
- Fleet monitoring
- Administration
- Communications
- Other operating requirements

The 40% assumption is a planning benchmark and will be refined after actual machine-level operating data, supplier quotations, insurance quotations, maintenance requirements and deployment costs are established.

---

12. NET OPERATING PROFIT DEFINITION

The model's "Net Operating Profit" represents:

Gross Revenue − Planning Operating Costs

It should not be interpreted as audited net income.

The model does not currently deduct separately for:

- Corporate income tax
- Depreciation
- Interest/financing costs
- Investor distributions
- Extraordinary losses
- Future expansion financing costs

These items should be incorporated into a more detailed financial model once the financing structure, final asset acquisition costs and operating data are available.

22. MANAGEMENT INTERPRETATION

The financial model demonstrates the potential operating economics of a 15-asset industrial fleet under increasing utilization.

The investment case is therefore based not only on equipment ownership, but on the ability to:

ACQUIRE SOUND ASSETS → DEPLOY QUICKLY → MAINTAIN HIGH AVAILABILITY → MONITOR OPERATIONS → CONTROL COSTS → IMPROVE UTILIZATION → REINVEST CASH FLOW → EXPAND CAPACITY

The model will become more precise as WIG moves from planning into procurement, contracting and deployment.

---

23. IMPORTANT MODEL NOTE

The revenue forecast is based on the long-term 15-asset industrial fleet and is presented as a planning scenario.

Phase 1 begins with 10 industrial assets plus one separate Hilux support vehicle.

The model should not be interpreted as saying that all 15 industrial assets are necessarily acquired on Day 1.

Progressive expansion will depend on:

- Commercial demand
- Utilization
- Cash generation
- Investor capital availability
- Asset procurement opportunities
- Operational performance
- Capital efficiency
