07 — ASSET ACQUISITION STRATEGY

WIG's strategy is to acquire sound, commercially viable assets at disciplined prices, rather than simply purchasing the newest or most expensive equipment.

Acquisition Framework

PRICE → CONDITION → PRODUCTIVE LIFE → MAINTENANCE → UTILIZATION → REVENUE → RESALE VALUE

Assets will be assessed based on:

- Purchase price
- Mechanical condition
- Expected productive operating life
- Maintenance requirements
- Revenue potential
- Parts availability
- Utilization potential
- Resale value

WIG buys with the future economic life of the asset in mind.

Where required, refurbishment and preventive maintenance will be incorporated into deployment planning.
