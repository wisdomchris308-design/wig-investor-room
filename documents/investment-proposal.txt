1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW

Wisdom Infrastructure Group (WIG) is building a technology-enabled industrial infrastructure and logistics platform focused on heavy equipment leasing, industrial haulage, project logistics and intelligent fleet operations.

WIG combines physical industrial assets with AI-enabled fleet technology, proactive commercial execution and operational resilience to address equipment availability, deployment delays, downtime, fuel visibility, asset security and fleet management challenges.

WIG is seeking USD $1.5 million in strategic capital to fund its Phase 1 deployment, establish a Smart Yard operating base, deploy fleet technology and create sufficient working-capital and operating reserves to support reliable operations.

9. ASSET ACQUISITION STRATEGY

WIG's acquisition philosophy is based on disciplined asset selection.

The company does not intend to pursue the newest or most expensive equipment simply because it is new.

The acquisition framework is:

PRICE → CONDITION → PRODUCTIVE LIFE → MAINTENANCE → UTILIZATION → REVENUE → RESALE VALUE

WIG intends to acquire commercially viable equipment with sound condition and meaningful remaining economic life, supported by proper maintenance and refurbishment where required.

The objective is to buy assets with the future economic life of the equipment in mind, while maintaining disciplined acquisition costs.

16. EMERGING COMMERCIAL OPPORTUNITY — POWATOM

WIG is currently engaged in active commercial discussions with POWATOM, a technology company specializing in electric drive systems and conversion solutions for three-wheelers and other mobility applications.

Discussions have progressed beyond an initial introduction into commercial and technical evaluation, including:

- POWATOM has provided WIG with a $500 distributor reference price for its conversion kit following commercial discussions.
- The proposed conversion kit is being evaluated for potential electric Keke/tricycle conversion opportunities in the Nigerian market.
- POWATOM has provided detailed battery requirements, including a 76.8V, 150Ah LFP traction-battery specification, enabling WIG to investigate local battery sourcing and/or assembly options.
- The parties have discussed potential customer-funded pilot deployments, installation, technical support, warranty considerations, shipping and after-sales support.
- WIG is evaluating local battery manufacturing/assembly options with the objective of improving customer affordability while maintaining the required technical specifications.
- WIG and POWATOM are working toward a potential formal commercial/distribution agreement, subject to final commercial, technical and contractual terms.

STRATEGIC RELEVANCE TO WIG

The opportunity supports WIG's broader strategy of developing an industrial technology distribution platform that can connect international technology providers with African markets through local commercial development, customer acquisition, deployment and after-sales support.
